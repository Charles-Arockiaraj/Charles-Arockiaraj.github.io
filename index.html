01Sheet metal · Manufacturing

Laser Cut File Automation

Problem
Every batch of parts had to be opened and cleaned up in AutoCAD, and the notches for the bending operator were drawn manually before the laser could cut it. It took around half a working day, and longer as the number of bends grew.
Solution
An Autodesk Inventor script that adds the bend notches, prepares the geometry and exports laser-ready files automatically, the same way every time.
Impact
The same batch is ready in under 30 minutes.

023D scanning · Inspection

Automatic Scan Alignment

Problem
3D scans had to be lined up by hand, picking matching points and nudging them into place by eye. One job took 1 to 3 hours, depending on the number of scans.
Solution
A Python automation that takes two 3D scans and aligns them with high-accuracy, 1:1 alignment.
Impact
Alignment finishes in under a minute, so scans are usable the same day they are captured.

03AI · Operations

Inventory Chat Assistant

Problem
Stock levels and prices live in a Google Sheet. Checking one item meant opening it, searching and scrolling through rows, which is slow on a phone.
Solution
A RAG assistant built in n8n that reads the inventory sheet and answers questions sent on WhatsApp or Telegram.
Impact
Anyone on the team gets a price or stock quantity by sending one message.
